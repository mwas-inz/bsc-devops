Telemetry is exported via OTLP to the OpenTelemetry Collector in the
observability namespace (otel-collector.observability.svc:4317).

Port-forward shortcuts:
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ include "bsc-devops.namespace" . }} svc/frontend 8080:80

Deploy with a specific image tag:
  helm upgrade --install {{ .Release.Name }} ./infrastructure/helm/bsc-devops \
    --namespace {{ include "bsc-devops.namespace" . }} --create-namespace \
    --set imageTag=<tag>
